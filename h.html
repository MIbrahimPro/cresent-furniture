Made slowly,
in solid wood.

A commission starts with two cups of chai and a pencil. We measure your room, sketch on paper, and walk the seasoning yard together to pick your timber. Then the workshop takes over — and you get photographs at every milestone, from first cut to final polish.

Plan a visit →
  1. I.

    Consult & measure

    We sit with your room — its light, its corners, its quirks — and sketch until the piece feels inevitable.

  2. II.

    Timber & drawing

    You approve the working drawing and choose your wood by hand. Nothing enters the workshop unchosen.

  3. III.

    Handcrafting

    Master carpenters cut, join and carve — with progress photos at every stage.

  4. IV.

    Finish & deliver

    Hand-rubbed finish, white-glove delivery and assembly — and free re-polishing for as long as you own it.

The house style.

Everything needed to build the website, social presence and print for Crescent — live on this page. Click swatches to copy hex codes; every asset downloads straight from the browser.

One arc, one name.

Recreated from the shop signboard: a terracotta sweep — the crescent — over Roman capitals, the descriptor set between hairline rules. The wordmark is set in Marcellus, the closest open match to the sign’s lettering. Website headlines use Fraunces; the logo stays its own thing.

Left — on paper, the everyday mark. Centre — on espresso, for dark surfaces. Right — the heritage tile, faithful to the physical sign, used only when the logo stands alone.

Compact lockup — navigation & small spaces

CCCC

Clear space — one capital C’s height, all four sides.

Minimums — 190 px / 35 mm for the lockup; below that, switch to the compact lockup or the arc alone. The physical sign carries a SINCE — strip whose year is unreadable in the reference photo; confirm it before adding it back.

Three voices.

    
Fraunces headlines — a soft old-style serif with real personality. Merriweather narrates the editorial moments — ledes, quotes, captions. Archivo does the quiet work — body, buttons, labels. The logo alone keeps Marcellus. All free on Google Fonts.

    

      
Aa

headlines & product names

        
  • Weight: 380, optical size auto
  • Accent: italic 340 in terracotta
  • Tracking: −.015em, leading 1.05
  • Never all-caps or bold

  • Never buttons or labels

Quiet parts.

    
Two-corner buttons (2px, barely) in ink; the ink button’s hover turns terracotta — the one color change you can feel. Underline links carry a dash-arrow that steps forward. Nothing else moves except photographs, at a slow 900ms.
